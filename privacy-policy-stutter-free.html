Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

StutterFree: Habit Changer ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

StutterFree is designed with privacy in mind. All exercises, progress data, and video journals are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on external servers.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data such as device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Analytics

We may use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics such as app opens, screen views, and feature usage. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify individual users.

Data Storage

All user-generated content, including video recordings, exercise progress, and habit tracking data, is stored exclusively on your device. We have no access to this data.

Children's Privacy

StutterFree is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics
  • RevenueCat — subscription management

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
